SHADOWLANTERN — a top-down stealth game: steal the Moonstone and reach the exit unseen. Slip through a lantern-lit manor at night, dodge the guards' vision cones, douse lanterns to make your own shadow, toss pebbles to pull patrols away, and vanish through the exit with the loot. Three manors, three guard kinds, ghost bonuses for never being seen.

## Run it

```sh
node serve.ts
```

Then open http://localhost:8080. Move with WASD or arrows, hold Shift to sneak, Space or E to interact / pick up loot / douse a lantern, Q to throw a pebble, P to pause, M to mute, R to restart.
